ILMA University Comprehensive Knowledge Base:

1. Departments and Programs:
- Faculty of Computer Science & Information Technology: BSCS, BSSE, MSCS, PhD CS.
- Faculty of Management Sciences: BBA, MBA, MS Management Sciences, PhD Management Sciences.
- Faculty of Media Science: BS Media Science.
- Faculty of Fashion Design: BS Fashion Design.

2. Campus Facilities:
- State-of-the-art Computer Labs with high-speed internet.
- A modern library with a vast collection of digital and physical resources.
- Auditorium for seminars and events.
- Student cafeteria and recreation areas.
- Dedicated placement and career counseling cell.

3. Admissions and Fees:
- Admissions open twice a year (Fall and Spring cycles).
- Merit-based and need-based scholarships are available for deserving students.
- Installment plans for tuition fees.

4. Student Life:
- Active student societies (Tech Society, Debating Club, Sports Club).
- Annual events like Global Conference on Management and Social Sciences (GCMSS).
- Industry visits and internships are mandatory parts of most programs.

5. Key Contacts:
- Admissions Office: ext 101, 102.
- Registrar Office: registrar@ilmauniversity.edu.pk.
- IT Support: support@ilmauniversity.edu.pk.
- Student Affairs: affairs@ilmauniversity.edu.pk.

6. Location:
Main Ibrahim Hydri Road, Korangi Creek, Karachi. It is located near the coastal area of Korangi.

7. Vision:
To be a leading center of excellence in education and research, producing graduates who can contribute to the global economy and society.

8. Accreditation:
Highest category (W) ranking by the Higher Education Commission (HEC). All programs are HEC recognized.
